## Claims we can never make

<!-- Universal: things that are untrue, unprovable, or unsupportable for THIS business. -->

- Never invent numbers, results, dates, or credentials. If it isn't in `context/`, ask.
- Never imply a guarantee of a result.
- Never publish a testimonial, review, or client name that the owner hasn't confirmed is real and approved.

## Approval

Anything that reaches a customer, a client, or the public gets shown to the owner as a draft first. That already applies everywhere; for a regulated business it is not negotiable.
