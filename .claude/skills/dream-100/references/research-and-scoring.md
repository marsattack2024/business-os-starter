# Research and scoring

## Source hierarchy

Prefer current first-party evidence:

1. the target's official site, business contact page, media kit, newsletter, podcast feed, profile, or recent public content;
2. public partnership, event, association, or sponsor pages;
3. reputable interviews and reporting;
4. third-party directories only as leads to verify.

Do not scrape behind login walls, evade platform controls, guess personal contact details, buy unverified lists, or treat AI-generated summaries as evidence.

## Candidate sources

- creators and media: podcast directories, guest archives, newsletters, public social profiles, event speakers, editorial contributors;
- local partners: associations, chambers, venue/vendor lists, event sponsors, community organizations, professional directories;
- buyers: current ICP evidence, public company material, role pages, relevant events, intent signals that are lawful and genuinely public;
- referrals: professionals serving the same customer before, during, or after the business's work.

## Weighted score

Score each dimension 0–5, then apply the weight.

| Dimension | Weight | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| Audience overlap | 30 | Is the relevant audience actually the one we serve? |
| Strategic/offer fit | 20 | Is there a credible mutually useful relationship? |
| Credibility/content quality | 15 | Would association help rather than harm trust? |
| Relationship/readiness | 15 | Is there evidence they collaborate, reply, refer, sponsor, or buy? |
| Access/timing | 10 | Is there an appropriate professional path and a current reason to engage? |
| Brand/compliance safety | 10 | Are the brand, claims, audience, channel, and jurisdiction acceptable? |

`weighted_score = sum((score / 5) * weight)`

No numerical total overrides a brand/compliance stop. Keep raw reach outside the weighted score so fame cannot compensate for poor fit.

## Tiers

- `ACTIVE_HIGH_UPSIDE`: up to 5 targets worth deeper research and a custom value asset.
- `ACTIVE_STRONG_FIT`: up to 10 targets with the clearest alignment.
- `ACTIVE_ACCESSIBLE`: up to 10 emerging, local, or relationship-ready targets.
- `WATCH`: verified but not ready, timely, or sufficiently supported.
- `RESEARCH`: promising lead with missing evidence.
- `CLOSED`: wrong fit, completed, or cooled down.
- `SUPPRESSED`: explicit no, opt-out, complaint, do-not-contact, or another binding stop.

## Evidence rules

- Record `observed_at` for every material claim.
- Distinguish reported reach from verified relevance. A follower count does not prove audience overlap.
- Use `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, or `LOW` confidence and say why.
- If the sender's audience size, results, proof, or relationship is not verified, remove it from the message or label it for owner confirmation.
- Recheck evidence before contact when the source is stale, the person changed roles, or the offer depends on current timing.

## Origins worth understanding

- Chet Holmes International describes concentrating on a small set of disproportionately valuable buyers and explicitly notes that a starter list can be smaller than 100: https://www.chetholmes.com/d100-double-your-sales/
- ClickFunnels describes the audience-owner adaptation—creators, communities, podcasts, and other places where the desired audience already gathers: https://www.clickfunnels.com/blog/the-dream-100/

Use these as strategic context, not as a fixed modern cadence or permission model.
