# Diagnosing low response

Do not assume silence means “send more.” Diagnose in this order.

## 1. Measurement

- Do we know the message was sent from the intended account?
- Does a provider receipt exist?
- Are delivery/read signals actually available and reliable?
- Are duplicate sends or stale contacts distorting the count?

If not, fix the instrument before drawing a copy conclusion.

## 2. Selection

- Is the audience overlap evidenced or merely assumed?
- Is this the correct person and role?
- Is the target active and current?
- Does the relationship mode match the target: buyer, referral partner, creator/media, or strategic partner?

Poor targeting cannot be repaired with a clever opening line.

## 3. Value

- Would the target or their audience receive something genuinely useful?
- Is the offer sized appropriately for the existing relationship?
- Are the terms honest about gifts, posts, usage rights, and compensation?
- Is the ask small enough to answer now?

## 4. Message

- Is the first line specific and verified?
- Is the draft short enough for the channel?
- Does it avoid generic praise, fake familiarity, jargon, and unsupported numbers?
- Is there one next step and an easy exit?

## 5. Channel and timing

- Is this the appropriate professional channel?
- Is the address/profile/form actually intended for this use?
- Do current platform rules, jurisdiction, consent, and preferences permit the touch?
- Is there evidence a secondary channel is appropriate, or would switching feel invasive?

## 6. Operations after a yes

If replies arrive but nothing progresses, inspect discovery, scheduling, ownership, deliverables, contracts, and follow-through. Enthusiasm is not an agreement and an agreement is not publication.

## Decision

- Fix evidence or targeting before rewriting.
- Test one new offer or message hypothesis at a time.
- Use the smallest reviewed batch.
- Respect explicit no/opt-out immediately.
- Use the campaign's evidence-based cadence and cooldown; close non-responsive targets rather than pursuing them across every channel.
