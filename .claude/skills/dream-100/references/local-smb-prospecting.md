# Local SMB prospecting cues

Adapted from public marketing-skill patterns (local website-status qualification).
Use when Dream 100 targets are **local small businesses** — shops, gyms, clinics,
salons, contractors, realtor offices, consultants with a service area.

This supplements [research-and-scoring.md](research-and-scoring.md). It does not
replace [channel-and-compliance.md](channel-and-compliance.md).

## Qualification axis: website status

| Status | Definition | Typical fit for web/marketing help |
|---|---|---|
| No site found | No credible standalone website after exact-name + city search | Hot |
| Social only | Facebook / Instagram / Linktree / marketplace only | Hot |
| Weak site | Thin, broken, non-mobile, or no clear contact/conversion | Warm |
| Has site | Credible modern standalone site | Cold unless another gap is documented |

## Discovery sources (manual research)

- Google Maps / Apple Maps / Bing Local — walk visible results; do **not** bulk-extract
- Yelp and local directories — secondary verification
- Facebook Pages — many SMBs are Facebook-first
- The business's own site and published contact channels

Cross-check every material claim with a source URL and date.

## Contact surfaces for this starter

Prefer published **business** contacts only:

- business email on the site or directory listing
- published business phone (voice — SMS needs separate lawful basis)
- Facebook Page messaging where rules allow
- Instagram professional/business profile where rules allow
- website partnership / contact form intended for vendors or general inquiries

Do **not**:

- bulk-scrape LinkedIn or Google Maps
- harvest personal emails, personal mobile numbers, or private profiles
- spray the same pitch across email, phone, Facebook, Instagram, and forms at once

## Simple local score

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Hot | No site or social-only + published phone + active business + in service area |
| Warm | Weak site or marketplace-only presence |
| Cold | Strong site already, or low-confidence evidence |
| Skip | Closed, duplicate, chain HQ without local authority, outside area |

## Compliance reminders

- Maps ToS prohibit bulk extraction — treat map browsing as research, not acquisition
- Public business phone ≠ SMS consent
- Silence is not permission to escalate channels
- Record source URLs for every contact field you keep
