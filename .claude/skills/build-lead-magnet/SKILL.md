# Build a Lead Magnet

A small useful thing, given away for an email address. Small and finished beats big and vague.

## Read first

- `context/customers.md` — what this person is stuck on
- `context/offer.md` — what they'll eventually buy, so the freebie leads there
- `context/gtm.md` — the angle we're already running (skip if empty)
- `context/voice.md`

## Steps

1. Offer the owner **three concepts**, one line each, and let them pick. Each must be something the customer could use within ten minutes of getting it — a checklist, a price guide, a one-page plan, a template, a short list of the mistakes everyone makes.

   Good test: does it solve one real problem completely? A "complete guide to everything" gets downloaded and never opened.

2. Ask for whatever only the owner knows — the real numbers, the actual steps, the thing they tell every customer on the phone. That knowledge is what makes it worth an email address.

3. Write the asset itself. Short. Formatted so it's usable, not impressive.

4. Write the copy that offers it:
   - The one-line promise
   - Three bullets of what's inside
   - The form line — what they type, what button says

5. Ask the owner where it goes: a section on the homepage, its own page, or a link in their bio. Build whichever they pick, following `update-website` or `build-offer-page`.

6. Tell them plainly what still needs a human: the email address collection itself needs an email tool connected. Point them at `connect-accounts` and say they can hand out the file manually until then. Don't pretend it's automated.

## Save it

`content/YYYY-MM-DD-lead-magnet-<name>.md` — the asset itself.
`content/YYYY-MM-DD-lead-magnet-<name>-copy.md` — the offer copy.

## Done when

- The asset is finished and usable on its own — not an outline.
- Every fact in it came from the owner or from `context/`.
- The owner knows exactly what happens next and what isn't connected yet.
