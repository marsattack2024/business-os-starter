# Measure Results

> **Better with connections.** Without connected accounts, ask the owner for the numbers and read what's in `content/` — that's still a real report. Run `connect-accounts` to have the numbers pulled automatically.

## Read first

- `context/goals.md` — the North Star. Every number gets judged against this, not against itself.
- `context/gtm.md` — what we said we'd do, so we can check whether we did it
- The last month of `content/` — what was actually made
- The last `measure-results` file, if there is one — the comparison is the whole point

## Steps

1. Ask what period they mean. Default to the last 30 days.

2. Gather what you can:
   - **Connected accounts** — pull the real numbers
   - **Not connected** — ask the owner directly: "How many enquiries did you get? How many turned into work? Roughly what did that come to?" Their answers are the most important numbers anyway; no dashboard shows revenue that arrived by text message.

3. Report **four numbers only**:
   - How many people found us
   - How many got in touch
   - How many became customers
   - What that was worth

   More than four and nobody reads it. If a number isn't available, write "not measured yet" rather than substituting something adjacent.

4. Say what moved and what didn't, compared to last time. If there is no last time, say so — this one is the baseline, and that's fine.

5. Name **one thing that worked** and one that didn't, with the evidence. "The pricing post brought 40 people and two enquiries; the ad brought 300 people and none."

6. Recommend **two moves**: do more of one thing, stop one thing. Stopping is the harder recommendation and usually the more valuable one.

7. Never dress up a bad month. An owner who trusts the good news needs the bad news to be honest too. If a number is genuinely unclear, say it's unclear.

## Save it

`content/YYYY-MM-DD-results.md` — the four numbers, what moved, and the two moves.

## Done when

- Four numbers are reported, with "not measured yet" wherever that's the truth.
- One thing that worked and one that didn't are named with evidence.
- There's something to do more of and something to stop.
