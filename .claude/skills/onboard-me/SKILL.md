# Onboard Me

Fills the gaps in `context/` by asking the owner, then writing their answers down. Takes about 15 minutes.

## Read first

All five files in `context/` — `business.md`, `customers.md`, `offer.md`, `voice.md`, `goals.md`. Most of these are already partly filled in.

## Steps

1. Find the gaps. Anything still written as `{{LIKE_THIS}}`, and anything that reads thin or generic, is a gap. Make a list.

2. Tell the owner what you already know about their business in three sentences, then say how many questions you have. This proves you did your homework and makes the questions feel short.

3. Ask **one question at a time**. Wait for the answer before the next one. Never send a numbered list of questions — this is a conversation, not a form.

4. Ask these four no matter what, because they change everything you write later:
   - "Paste me something you actually wrote to a customer — an email, a text, a post. Anything real." (goes into `context/voice.md`, word for word, untouched)
   - "What's the one thing you'd never say about your business? Words, claims, promises — anything that's not you." (goes into the **Never** section of `context/voice.md`)
   - "If your business changed one thing about your life this year, what would it be?" (goes into `context/goals.md`, in their words)
   - "What's the first job you'd hand a great assistant?" (goes into `context/goals.md`)

5. Write each answer into the matching `context/` file **as you go** — don't wait until the end. Keep the owner's own phrasing wherever it's good. Do not smooth it into consultant language.

6. When the owner says something they "should really do," add it to the suggestion pool in `context/goals.md`.

7. Read back a short summary: who they serve, what they sell, how they sound, what they're aiming at. Ask "did I get anything wrong?" Fix whatever they correct, in the file, right then.

## Save it

`content/YYYY-MM-DD-onboarding-notes.md` — the questions you asked and the answers you got, so nothing is lost if a file gets edited later.

## Done when

- No `{{TOKENS}}` are left in any `context/` file.
- `context/voice.md` contains a real sample the owner actually wrote.
- The owner has heard the read-back and confirmed it.
