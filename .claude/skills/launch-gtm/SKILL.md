# Launch the Go-To-Market

Researches the owner's market and writes the plan for winning customers. Every other writing skill reads the result, so this is worth doing properly.

**Tell the owner up front:** this takes 10–20 minutes and they'll see a lot of research scroll past. They don't need to read it. When it's done, you'll walk them through it in plain English.

## Read first

- `context/business.md`, `context/offer.md`, `context/customers.md` — what we sell and to whom
- `context/goals.md` — what the owner is actually aiming at
- `context/voice.md` — so the plan sounds like them

## Steps

1. Ask two questions and wait for both:
   - "Who else near you does something like this?" (names or websites, whatever they have)
   - "What do people ask you right before they buy?"

2. Research. Read the owner's own website and socials. Look up the competitors they named and two more you find. Look for what customers in this market complain about, search for, and say in reviews. Gather real specifics — prices, phrases people actually use, gaps nobody is filling.

3. Write the plan with exactly these parts:
   - **The one customer** — one real person, described so specifically the owner recognizes them
   - **The offer, in one sentence** — what they get, what it costs, why now
   - **Three reasons to believe it** — proof, not adjectives
   - **Where these customers actually are** — the two or three places worth showing up, and why the others aren't
   - **The first three campaigns** — each with the angle, the channel, and the first thing to make
   - **The next 30 days** — week by week, one main thing per week

4. Every claim comes from the research or from `context/`. If something is a guess, write "guess:" in front of it. A wrong fact here spreads into everything written afterwards.

5. Copy the short version — the one customer, the offer sentence, the channels, the first campaign — into `context/gtm.md`, replacing what's there.

6. Read back the offer sentence and the first campaign out loud to the owner. Ask "does this sound like your business?" Fix what they push back on, in both files.

## Save it

`content/YYYY-MM-DD-gtm-plan.md` — the full plan.
`context/gtm.md` — the short version other skills read.

## Done when

- `context/gtm.md` has no `{{TOKENS}}` and names one specific customer.
- The first three campaigns each name a channel and a first thing to make.
- The owner has heard the offer sentence and said yes to it.
