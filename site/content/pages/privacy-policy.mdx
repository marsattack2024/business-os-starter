[Studio Name] respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we
collect through this website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the
choices you have.

## Information we collect

- **Information you give us.** Your name, email address, phone number, and
  anything you write in an inquiry, quiz, or scheduling form on this site.
- **Information collected automatically.** Standard web data such as your IP
  address, browser and device type, referring page, and how you moved through
  the site. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies.

## How we use your information

We use it to respond to your inquiry, schedule and deliver your session,
communicate about your booking, run and measure our marketing, keep the site
secure, and meet our legal and recordkeeping obligations.

## Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to make the site work, remember your
preferences, measure traffic, and understand how our marketing performs. You can
control or block cookies through your browser settings; some parts of the site
may not work as well if you do.

## Automated and AI-assisted tools

We use software that includes automated and AI-assisted tools to help run the
studio. They help us organize and summarize inquiries, draft follow-up messages,
schedule sessions, and prepare internal reporting. Information you submit through
this website may be processed by those tools.

A person reviews anything that affects you before we act on it. These tools
support our team rather than replace it, we do not use them to make automated
decisions about you without human involvement, and we do not sell your personal
information.

## How we share information

We do **not** sell your personal information. We share it only with service
providers who help us run the studio, and only as much as they need to provide
that service: our client-management system, our email and messaging
provider, our analytics and advertising providers, and the host that serves this
website. We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to
protect the studio against fraud or abuse.

Third-party tools embedded on this site are governed by their own privacy
policies. Review those policies before submitting information through them.

## Data retention

We keep inquiry and client information for as long as we need it to serve you
and to meet our business, tax, and legal obligations, then delete or anonymize
it.

## Your rights and choices

You can ask us to update, correct, or delete the information you submitted
through this site. You can unsubscribe from marketing email at any time using
the link in any message. Depending on where you live, you may have additional
rights under your local privacy law.

To make a request, contact us at [Email].

## Children's privacy

This site is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect
information from children. Where a session involves a minor, we work through the
parent or guardian.

## Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we hold. No method of
transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute
security.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site, the studio's operations, or the tools we
use change. The updated version will be posted on this page.

## Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to [Email].
