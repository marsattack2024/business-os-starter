This file is here to show you how publishing works. It is the only file in
`content/` that your website is showing right now.

Look at the top of this file, between the two lines of three dashes. The line
that says `published: true` is the switch. Change it to `false`, save, and this
post comes off your site. Delete this file and it disappears entirely.

## Why the other files are not here

Your employee saves everything it makes into `content/` — emails, plans,
notes, weekly reviews. Those are working documents, not blog posts, so they do
not have that switch and your website ignores them.

## What to do next

Ask your employee to **write a blog post**. It will save the post here with the
switch turned on, and it will show up on your homepage and at `/blog` within a
second or two. Then delete this example file.
